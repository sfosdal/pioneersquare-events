Sources

Where this calendar’s listings come from.

Ticketing feeds

Ticketmaster, room by room — Baba Yaga (its TicketWeb shows), Lumen Field, T-Mobile Park and WAMU Theater (stadium tours, parking and memberships left out). Showbox SoDo from Showbox Presents' own listing (AXS), which also says when a night is sold out or postponed. Resale-only listings are never shown.

Venue calendars

The rooms' own pages: the Central Saloon's calendar, the Seattle Jazz Fellowship's and Nordo's Squarespace calendars, Trinity's weekend parties through their Eventbrite links, Baba Yaga's weekly nights from ma.to. Occidental Square from the Downtown Seattle Association's calendar, and everything else in the neighbourhood — galleries, museums, walks, pop-ups — from the Alliance for Pioneer Square's calendar (pioneersquare.org), filed under Around Pioneer Square unless a room has a row of its own, and its First Thursday Art Walk pages. Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park from the National Park Service's events API. Waterfront Park (Pier 62) from the cards on Friends of Waterfront Park's events page. Owl 'n Thistle's weekly music table, written out ten weeks ahead. March to the Match at Occidental Square before every Sounders home game, from the club's schedule.

Game dates

ESPN for the Seahawks, Storm, Reign and Sounders, MLB’s stats API for the Mariners (first-pitch times and the standings), and the NHL’s for the Kraken. Home games at Lumen Field and T-Mobile Park are in the listing; the Kraken and Storm play up at Climate Pledge Arena, so their games are in the Teams view only.

The map

The shoreline, the lakes and the main roads are © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), drawn here as one file made at build time — no map tiles, nothing fetched from anywhere else. Venues are placed from their street addresses.

The final word

Refreshed once a day at noon. Every listing links to the venue or ticket page — check there before you go.
